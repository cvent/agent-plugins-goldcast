# Follow-up email

Read this after you've chosen one angle (SKILL.md Step 5). The fidelity rules in Step 6 apply to everything here.

## Segment

Write for the segment confirmed in intake:

- **Attendees:** thank them in one line, give the single most useful idea from the session, point to the recording to rewatch or share.
- **Registered no-shows:** don't make them feel guilty. Say what they missed in terms of the value (the idea, not "you missed out"), and point to the recording.
- **Both, or unclear:** write a neutral version that works for either (no "thanks for joining" and no "sorry we missed you"). Offer to produce segment-specific versions as the one next step.

Never name or quote attendees, and never use registrant data.

## Structure

1. **Subject lines.** Give 3 options, each under about 50 characters, different in approach (benefit, curiosity, direct). Avoid all caps and spam-trigger punctuation.
2. **Preview text.** One line under about 90 characters that adds to the subject instead of repeating it.
3. **Greeting.** Use `[MERGE: first name]` unless the user gave their own merge syntax.
4. **Body (80 to 160 words).** One short opening line, then the angle: one to three points from the session that a speaker actually made, in plain language. A verbatim quote is optional, at most one, attributed with name, title, and company from `list_speakers`.
5. **One CTA.** Default is to watch the recording (on-demand link or `[LINK: on-demand recording]`). If the user gave a goal (next event, slides, demo), use that instead, and don't add a second competing CTA.
6. **Sign-off.** `[SENDER NAME]` and `[SENDER TITLE]` placeholders unless the user supplied them.

## Format

- Plain, conversational, short paragraphs. No headers. One link-styled CTA line.
- Don't paste long transcript passages. Don't include speaker emails or any attendee data.
- Anything the user must fill in goes in `[MERGE: ...]` or `[LINK: ...]` and in Review notes.

## Multiple sessions

One email around the through-line, with links per session only if the user supplied them. Don't list every session unless asked.
